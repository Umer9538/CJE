## Changes from approved build 13
- **Login/store fixes preserved:** Apple sign-in with nonce and hidden-email/name handling, Apple buttons/capability, Google server client ID, email-verification refresh and mandatory first-launch terms. Apple account deletion reauthenticates the same user and revokes its Apple authorization.
- **Personal privacy onboarding:** optional phone; age band without birth date; the existing 13+ service eligibility rule retained. Versioned terms and privacy acknowledgements are recorded per account. Legacy/CSV-imported accounts complete this personally. Under-16 activation requires actual parental authorization verified by an administrator; a checkbox alone is insufficient.
- **Access control:** verified, active, onboarded accounts only; county, school, role and draft restrictions enforced by the server. Superadmin county/all-counties selection remains. Participant selection uses a minimal directory instead of exposing email, device tokens or disciplinary records. Queries and quarterly BEX reporting are aligned with those boundaries.
- **UGC safety:** your report/block functionality preserved and extended to initiatives/comments; unblock screen and county-scoped BEX/superadmin moderation queue added. Bounded reasons, parent validation, a basic text filter and visible failures replace silent success. Individual anonymous poll choices are protected; participation can remain visible.
- **Integrity/deletion:** server-validated atomic voting prevents duplicate/count forgery. Meeting deletion checks actual attendance, not an eventual counter. Account deletion is accessible even before approval, email verification or privacy/profile setup; it erases Auth/profile/photo and linked personal records. Retained organisational authorship is de-identified, not a guarantee that every free-text attachment is anonymous. Failed erasures retry through a server-only queue. Retention uses meeting dates and preserves attendance evidence through the school-year boundary.
- **Platform/policy:** Android SDK 36, unnecessary audio permission removed, 16 KB native alignment checked. iOS 15.5 minimum, non-alpha icon and matching Google/Firebase IDs retained; APNs entitlements added. App and public RO/EN policy/terms use **2026-09-30**, including ML Kit technical telemetry and deletion instructions.
- **Legal clarification, 1 October:** app/public wording distinguishes the actual controller from future institutional roles, limits the contract basis to necessary processing under a valid user contract, and separates under-16 GDPR consent from minors' contractual capacity. Real-pupil use requires documented institutional activation and lawful bases. Permanent-link risk is disclosed. No functionality, data categories, purposes or controller identity changed; acknowledgement IDs remain **2026-09-30**, matching the live backend. The public page separately dates the clarification.
